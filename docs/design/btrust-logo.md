## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final app logo asset for B-Trust Bank, a microbanking and interest management web application. Create one polished, extremely simple icon-only logo to replace its generic gold temple icon. Direction: a strong geometric capital B monogram that subtly suggests two interlocking protective loops, conveying trust and connection. A single bold warm-gold mark on a flat near-charcoal rounded-square tile. Palette: charcoal #20221f and warm gold #f4cc65. Flat vector-like raster artwork, crisp precise edges, balanced negative space, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D. Square composition, tile centered and fills approximately 94% of the canvas with a genuinely transparent background outside the rounded corners. The B must remain instantly readable at 24px to 40px. No fine detail, no building or temple, no wordmark, no slogans, no extra text, no additional logo variations, no mockup or presentation sheet, no watermark. Deliver only the finished single logo tile on transparency.
